<h2>TAILORED CARE</h2>

Pet & Home care is our full-time priority, not an afterthought. <br>
**We learn your needs**<br>
Before each booking, we send a **complete questionnaire** to understand your pet’s routine, medical requirements, your household rules, and any specific instructions for your home or smallholding. We also offer a **video meet & greet** before booking, so you can get to know us from anywhere.<br>

**Your routine becomes ours**

We **structure our day around your needs**: morning walks and playtime for your pets, daily gardening, cleaning, and security checks for your home. For farm sits, we handle feeding and livestock care. We follow your schedule and handle the unexpected, so you can enjoy your time away knowing everything is under control.<br>

**You stay informed**
We send **daily photo and video updates**, so you can work, relax, or travel knowing your pets are safe, your home is secure, and everything is running smoothly. In case of an unexpected event, we follow an **emergency protocol**: we contact your vet immediately, manage the situation calmly, and notify you right away.<br>
